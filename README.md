# KharchaWise

KharchaWise is a simple app to track your money. It helps you see how much you earn and how much you spend. The design is clean and easy to use.

## Features

**Dashboard Overview**  
The main page shows your total money at the top. Right next to it, you can see your total income and total expenses. You do not have to click anywhere else to see your balance.

**Adding Transactions**  
You can easily log new expenses or income. There is a built-in calculator so you can type numbers fast. You can also pick a clear category for your expense, like food or rent.

**Search and Filters**  
If you want to find an old record, you can just search for it. You can also filter by category or transaction type. This makes finding your history very fast.

**Smart Analytics**  
The app has colorful charts. A pie chart shows where you spend the most money. A bar chart compares your income and your expenses month by month.

**Spending Insights**  
The app looks at your data and gives you simple tips. It tells you your top expense category. It also compares this month's spending to last month.

**Budgets**  
You can set limits on how much you want to spend in a category. The app will track your spending to see if you stay inside your budget or go over it.

**Works on Phones**  
You can use the app on your computer or on your phone perfectly. It fits on any screen size and feels just like a real mobile app.

## Tech Stack
- HTML5
- CSS3
- JavaScript
- Chart.js
- Firebase Realtime Database
- Firebase Authentication
